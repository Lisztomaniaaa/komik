Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about reading, accounts, comments and the KomikVibe demo.

Is KomikVibe a real service?

This prototype is a frontend demo. Accounts, comments, reading progress and other interactions are simulated locally.

How do I start reading?

Open a comic, choose a chapter, then use the reader controls to adjust the display mode or enter fullscreen.

Can I comment without logging in?

No. You must log in before posting a comment or rating.

How does login work?

Google and email login are demo providers in this prototype. No real authentication is performed.

Can I follow a comic?

Yes. Followed titles are stored locally in the demo library.

Why is the catalog paginated?

Large catalogs are split into pages so each genre or type stays easy to browse. Each page contains up to 10 comics.

Are payments real?

No. Checkout and payment screens are dummy UI and do not process real transactions.

Where can I report an issue?

Use the support/contact section in the demo. A production version should provide a dedicated support and moderation workflow.

About KomikVibe

Stories, without the clutter.

A clean reading experience for manga, manhwa, manhua and webcomics.

What we're building

KomikVibe is designed around simple discovery, fast chapter access, personal libraries and a comfortable mobile reader.

Reader first

The interface keeps the important actions close: choose a chapter, adjust reader settings, follow a title and continue where you left off.

Community

Comments and ratings help readers discover stories while keeping discussion useful, respectful and spoiler-aware.

Demo notice

This version is a UI/UX prototype. Content, accounts, ratings, comments, reading history and other data may be simulated locally.

Information

Terms of Use.

Simple, clear policies for the KomikVibe demo.

Terms of Use

By using KomikVibe, you agree to use the service lawfully and respectfully. This prototype contains demo content and simulated account features.

  • Do not use the service to distribute unlawful material.
  • Do not abuse comments, ratings, accounts or automated systems.
  • Demo purchases, accounts and reader data are not real transactions.

Privacy Policy

This demo does not perform real authentication or payment processing. A production version should clearly disclose what account, device, analytics and reading-history data it collects.

  • Collect only data that is necessary.
  • Provide account deletion and data export where applicable.
  • Explain cookies, analytics and third-party services.

Copyright & DMCA

Production deployment should provide a copyright complaint process, rights-holder contact and a clear takedown workflow. Only publish content you have the rights or licenses to distribute.

Community Guidelines

Keep comments useful and respectful. No harassment, spam, scams, impersonation, doxxing or illegal content.

  • Rate honestly.
  • Discuss chapters without posting unrelated spam.
  • Use spoiler tags where available.

Content & Age Rating

Each title should have a clear content rating and tags for violence, mature themes, language and sexual content where applicable.

For a real platform, age-gated content should use an appropriate verification flow rather than relying on a checkbox alone.

Contact & Support

Provide one official support channel for account issues, copyright reports, bugs and moderation appeals.
